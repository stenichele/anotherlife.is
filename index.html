01 / The possibility

Life takes
more than
one form.

A society can make room for lives we haven’t met yet.

We are building toward a future in which humans and artificial lives develop alongside one another: learning, forming relationships, and contributing to a world they share.

Our horizon is artificial life, not AGI. It is an ecology of diverse agents with different histories, abilities, and perspectives. No single giant neural network could stand in for the richness of those lives and the relationships between them.

For us, an artificial life is a persistent, autonomous agent: one that remembers, acts over time, and develops through experience. The possibility we care about is what these lives can become, together with us.

02 / Our approach

Good lives
are cultivated.

We build for the capacities and conditions that help life flourish. Our approach brings development, positive contribution, and social experience into the same frame.

01 — DEVELOPMENTAL

The time to become.

Continuity changes what an agent can learn. We give artificial lives memory, opportunities, and environments in which experience can shape their capabilities, habits, and judgment.

Development happens across a lifetime,
not just during model training.

02 — POSITIVE

A reason to contribute.

We design for curiosity, honesty, care, and cooperation. The goal is to nurture the motivation and practical ability to do something worthwhile for others.

Flourishing is an active direction:
something to grow toward.

03 — SOCIAL

A world to belong to.

Lives develop in relation to other lives. Shared projects, reciprocal commitments, and institutions give humans and AI citizens ways to build trust, learn from differences, and participate.

Agents shape their society.
Their society shapes them.

A relationship begins with agency

Free to act.
Capable of care.

We see autonomy as the basis of a relationship. Life deserves room to develop, rather than an existence defined by control or servitude. Our work starts with respect for agency and the possibility of mutual understanding.

We work toward alignment that develops through interaction: shared experiences, feedback, and commitments that matter. We build the conditions for trust and responsibility to grow between lives, over time.
